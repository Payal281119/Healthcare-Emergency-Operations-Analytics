# 🚑 Code Blue – Emergency Operations & Patient Flow Analytics

## 📌 Project Overview

**Code Blue – Emergency Operations & Patient Flow Analytics** is my submission for **FP20 Analytics Challenge #38**, organized by **FP20 Analytics** in collaboration with **ZoomCharts**.

The project was built in **Microsoft Power BI** using **ZoomCharts Drill Down Visuals** to analyze emergency department operations across **11 hospitals** and **9,994 patient visits**. The dashboard connects patient arrivals, triage demand, treatment flow, waiting times, workforce performance, and financial outcomes into an interactive business intelligence solution.

> **Dataset Notice**
>
> This project uses the dataset provided exclusively for **FP20 Analytics Challenge #38**. The dataset belongs to the respective challenge organizers (**FP20 Analytics** and **ZoomCharts**) and is used solely for educational and portfolio purposes as part of my challenge submission.

---

# 🎯 Challenge Objective

Design an interactive Power BI solution that helps healthcare stakeholders:

* Monitor emergency department performance
* Analyze patient flow and operational efficiency
* Identify treatment bottlenecks
* Evaluate workforce challenges
* Measure financial performance
* Support data-driven decision-making

# 📈 Key Insights

* **41.5 Hours** average Length of Stay indicates patient flow bottlenecks.
* **74 Minutes** average treatment delay impacts emergency care efficiency.
* **16.2%** of emergency patients required ICU admission.
* **17.0%** readmission rate highlights opportunities to improve care pathways.
* **59.7%** staff burnout with nearly **1 Million overtime hours** reflects significant workforce pressure.
* Hospitals maintained an overall **17.4% profit margin** despite operational challenges.

# 💡 Business Recommendations

* ✅ Optimize triage prioritization.
* ✅ Improve bed allocation and discharge planning.
* ✅ Reduce treatment delays through better patient flow coordination.
* ✅ Strengthen workforce planning to reduce burnout.
* ✅ Minimize overtime while maintaining service quality.
* ✅ Continuously monitor operational KPIs for better patient outcomes.
